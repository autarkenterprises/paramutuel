Resolver field help
If set, only that address may call resolve() and retract(). You remain the on-chain proposer. Leave empty to default resolver to your connected wallet.
Closer role help
closeBetting() can stop bets before the scheduled time. closeResolutionWindow() can close the resolver window early (after betting ends), allowing anyone to call expire(). Leave a closer field empty to disable that authority role. To delegate a closer role to the proposer, enter the same wallet address you are connected with when creating the wager. No-max windows require explicit closer addresses.
Time-window help
Betting close can be relative (now + seconds) or absolute (local date/time converted to unix timestamp). Resolution window can also be relative (seconds after betting close) or absolute (a local date/time converted into a duration after betting close). No-max toggles set the on-chain value to 0, which requires the corresponding closer role. In absolute resolution mode, the selected close time must be after betting close by at least the factory minimum resolution window.
Seed liquidity help
Optional initial liquidity at create-time. Provide aligned index and amount lists (for example: indices 0,2 and amounts 10,3.5). The dApp approves the total once and submits one create transaction.
