About Paramutuel

Parimutuel pools for propositions and events — stake on outcomes with a wallet, settle on-chain, claim from the pot.

What you can do here

Use Place a bet or the bet page for a short path: connect a wallet, approve collateral, and stake on outcomes. The Explorer lists markets from the indexer; the full App adds create, resolve, claim, and odds preview for people comfortable with more controls.

Testnet and mainnet

The strip under the navigation shows which network this deployment uses — for example Base Sepolia (testnet) for practice, or Base mainnet when the site is configured for production.

Testnet is real smart-contract behavior with tokens that have no monetary value; it stays useful for trying the flow even after mainnet is live. Mainnet involves real assets: always confirm the network in your wallet matches the banner before you sign anything.

Base Sepolia (common testnet): chain ID 84532, default public RPC endpoint https://sepolia.base.org.

Developers

The protocol, static site, and app are open source. Clone or browse the repo for Solidity contracts, the self-custody dApp, indexer, and tooling.

  • github.com/autarkenterprises/paramutuel

This public site focuses on participants. Technical documentation for running infrastructure lives in the repository for people who deploy their own environments.

Risks

Smart contracts and resolver choices can behave in ways you do not expect. Only use funds you can afford to lose, verify addresses (factory and wager) on a block explorer, and read each transaction in your wallet before approving.
