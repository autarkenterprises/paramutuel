About Paramutuel

Paramutuel provides permissionless, real crypto, blockchain-powered prop betting. Anyone can propose any wager, in any ERC-20 token, with configurable resolution criteria. Proposers, bettors, and resolvers interact on-chain, through a fully distributed protocol, without relying on centralized platforms to approve wagers, or authorize payouts. Paramutuel is designed for agents, whether human or artificial: all aspects of the wager lifecycle are fully configurable, enabling a wide variety of uses — friendly bets, forecasting current events, sports betting, and more.

The Paramutuel protocol

These principles are implemented as one on-chain system: factory-deployed wagers, shared pool per market, resolver-driven settlement, and pro-rata claims in the collateral token the market chose. Markets are either enumerated (fixed outcome labels with bitmask tickets and payoff policies, including batch placeBets) or freeform (open text answers at stake time; resolver publishes the exact winning string).

  • Enumerated — Up to 255 labeled outcomes; tickets are bitmasks with configurable payoff rules (single-winner, any-of, exact-set, at-least-k, weighted overlap).
  • Freeform — No fixed list at create time; stakes are keyed by a deterministic id derived from the bettor’s UTF-8 answer.

Technical reference: docs/MACHINE.md and ADRs in research/adr/.

What you can do here

Use Propose a Wager for guided creation (templates and assisted flows in development), or Place a Bet to find wagers and stake with a wallet. The stake page uses the Paramutuel wager ABI: enumerated markets use placeBet / placeBets; freeform markets use placeBet(string,uint256) with your exact answer text. Direct links like bet.html?wager=0x… open the same flow. The Explorer lists wagers from the indexer; the full App bundles create, resolve, claim, and odds preview.

Testnet and mainnet

The strip under the navigation shows which network this deployment uses — for example Base Sepolia (testnet) for practice, or Base mainnet when the site is configured for production.

Testnet is real smart-contract behavior with tokens that have no monetary value; it stays useful for trying the flow even after mainnet is live. Mainnet involves real assets: always confirm the network in your wallet matches the banner before you sign anything.

Base Sepolia (common testnet): chain ID 84532, default public RPC endpoint https://sepolia.base.org.

Risks

Smart contracts and resolver choices can behave in ways you do not expect. Only use funds you can afford to lose, verify addresses (factory and wager) on a block explorer, and read each transaction in your wallet before approving.
