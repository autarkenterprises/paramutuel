Get started

Paramutuel is on-chain parimutuel betting: one pool per wager, odds move as people stake on outcomes, then the resolver settles and winners claim pro-rata.

  1. Wallet & network Install a browser wallet (e.g. MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet). Match the chain shown in the top banner (today: Base Sepolia testnet unless you changed config/deployments.json).
  2. Gas & collateral Fund the wallet with Sepolia ETH on Base for gas. For betting, use the ERC-20 shown in the app for each wager (USDC-style presets exist in the dApp). Amounts in the feed are raw token units (smallest decimals).
  3. Stake on a market Use the Place a bet page (from the live feed or Explorer) for a short path: connect wallet, enter amounts per outcome, approve once, and submit placeBets. Or use the full dApp for create, odds preview, resolve, and claim.
  4. Inspect & verify Use Explorer (auto-refreshes while the tab is visible) to search propositions and open the bet page from any wager row. Use the block explorer link in the top banner to verify contract code and transactions for the active chain.
  5. Risks Smart contracts and resolver decisions carry risk; only use funds you can afford to lose. Double-check factory and wager addresses before depositing.

Create Wagers

Deploy parimutuel wagers for any proposition with custom outcomes, time windows, and fee structures. Supports templates for sports events, elections, and long-horizon forecasts.

Place Bets

Bet on outcomes using ERC-20 collateral. Single bets or batch multi-outcome legs in one transaction. Live odds preview shows expected payouts before you commit.

Resolve & Claim

Wagers resolve to a winning outcome, retract (refund minus fees), or expire if unresolved. Winners claim pro-rata payouts from the net pot.

Why Paramutuel

Transparent pools and clear rules on-chain: suitable whenever you want verifiable stakes, moving odds, and settlement without a centralized book.

Advantages

  • Parimutuel math — all stakes go into one pot per market; implied odds reflect aggregate sentiment, not a house line.
  • ERC-20 collateral — use standard tokens (e.g. USDC) per wager; amounts are enforced by contract logic.
  • Composable lifecycle — factory-deployed wagers with configurable closers, resolution windows, and fee recipients (treasury + extras).
  • Indexer + explorer — HTTP APIs and tables for bots, operators, and UIs; no bespoke scraping.
  • Base-first — low-friction L2 environment aligned with retail and builder tooling (see repo research memos on chain choice).

Use cases

  • Prop and event markets — sports, elections, product launches, KPI bets between teams or communities.
  • Long-horizon forecasts — templates for extended betting and resolution windows.
  • Closer-managed windows — authority-driven close when fixed deadlines are a poor fit.
  • Charity or beneficiary splits — fee configuration supports directing a share of the pot to treasuries or named recipients (within protocol caps).
  • Integration & automation — MCP/CLI workflows and resolution services for operators (see docs/MACHINE.md, docs/WORKFLOWS.md).

How It Works

  1. Create — A proposer deploys a wager with a proposition, outcomes, and time windows via the factory contract.
  2. Bet — Anyone deposits ERC-20 collateral on an outcome during the betting window.
  3. Resolve — The resolver selects the winning outcome (or retracts/expires).
  4. Claim — Winners claim pro-rata payouts; fee recipients withdraw accrued fees.
