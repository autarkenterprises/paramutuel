Create Markets

Deploy paramutuel markets for any question with custom outcomes, time windows, and fee structures. Supports templates for sports events, elections, and long-horizon forecasts.

Place Bets

Bet on outcomes using ERC-20 collateral. Single bets or batch multi-outcome legs in one transaction. Live odds preview shows expected payouts before you commit.

Resolve & Claim

Markets resolve to a winning outcome, retract (refund minus fees), or expire if unresolved. Winners claim pro-rata payouts from the net pot.

How It Works

  1. Create — A proposer deploys a market with a question, outcomes, and time windows via the factory contract.
  2. Bet — Anyone deposits ERC-20 collateral on an outcome during the betting window.
  3. Resolve — The resolver selects the winning outcome (or retracts/expires).
  4. Claim — Winners claim pro-rata payouts; fee recipients withdraw accrued fees.
