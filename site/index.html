How wagers move from idea to payout

Each wager is its own on-chain contract: one shared pool, moving odds, and fixed rules for when betting stops and who may settle. Deploy through a factory for your market shape: classic enumerated (single-outcome stakes), bitmask enumerated (richer payoff rules and batch tickets), or freeform (bettors type answers; resolver picks the exact winning string).

  1. 1

    Create

    A proposer deploys a wager through the factory: the question, collateral token, fee split, and time rules. Enumerated markets include an explicit outcome list (up to 255 labels). Freeform markets ship without enumerated outcomes; stakes are keyed by the UTF-8 text each bettor chooses. The resolver defaults to the proposer unless another address is set.

  2. 2

    Open betting

    Anyone with the right token stakes: on outcome indices (classic enumerated), on ticket bitmasks (including multi-leg batch bets), or with a text answer (freeform). All stakes sit in one pot; implied odds shift as the pool grows.

  3. 3

    Close betting

    New bets stop when the scheduled time is reached or a designated betting closer ends the window early—whichever comes first.

  4. 4

    Settle

    During the resolution window, the resolver settles: winning outcome index (classic enumerated), winning ticket mask (bitmask enumerated, per the wager’s payoff rule), or the exact winning answer string (freeform). They may retract or let the window lapse; then anyone can call expire so participants recover stakes (fees may still apply).

  5. 5

    Claim

    After settlement, winners withdraw pro-rata payouts; on retract or expire, refunds follow the contract rules. Fee recipients claim their configured share.

Full state machine, role permissions, and timer vs authority behavior: docs/WAGER-LIFECYCLE.md in the open-source repo.

Protocol & getting started

The Paramutuel protocol is on-chain parimutuel betting: one pool per wager, odds move as people stake, then the resolver settles and winners claim pro-rata. Deployments share the same lifecycle (open → close → resolve / retract / expire → claim) but differ in how tickets are expressed: classic single-outcome legs, bitmask tickets with payoff policies such as single-winner, any-of, exact-set, at-least-k, and weighted overlap, and freeform user-typed answers hashed on-chain. Use Propose a Wager or Place a Bet for the main flows; the full dApp exposes factory parameters.

  1. Wallet & network Install a browser wallet (e.g. MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet). Use the same network as the strip under the navigation (often Base Sepolia for practice, or Base mainnet when this site is configured for production). For Base Sepolia testnet: chain ID 84532, default public RPC https://sepolia.base.org — add it as a custom network if your wallet does not list Base Sepolia already.
  2. Gas & collateral Fund the wallet with Sepolia ETH on Base for gas. For betting, use the ERC-20 shown in the app for each wager (USDC-style presets exist in the dApp). Amounts in the feed are raw token units (smallest decimals).
  3. Stake on a wager Use Place a Bet to search open wagers and open the wallet flow, skim the live feed, or use Explorer. Connect your wallet: enumerated markets use placeBet / placeBets; freeform markets use a text answer plus placeBet(string,uint256). The dApp adds create, odds preview, resolve, and claim across Paramutuel deployments.
  4. Inspect & verify Use Explorer (auto-refreshes while the tab is visible) to search propositions and open the bet page from any wager row. Use the block explorer link in the top banner to verify contract code and transactions for the active chain.
  5. Power users & builders See About → Developers for agents, MCP, indexer API, and the open-source repo. Contract lifecycle reference: WAGER-LIFECYCLE.md.
  6. Risks Smart contracts and resolver decisions carry risk; only use funds you can afford to lose. Double-check factory and wager addresses before depositing.

Create Wagers

Deploy through the factory that fits your market: classic enumerated outcomes, bitmask enumerated markets with advanced payoff rules and up to 255 outcomes, or freeform text-answer pools with no fixed list at create time.

Place Bets

Stake ERC-20 collateral per outcome index, per bitmask ticket (including batched legs), or with a freeform UTF-8 answer. Odds and pool share update as the pot moves.

Resolve & Claim

Resolvers commit the winning index, bitmask, or exact string (freeform); retract and expire paths refund per contract rules. Winners claim pro-rata from the net pot.

Why Paramutuel

Transparent pools and clear rules on-chain: suitable whenever you want verifiable stakes, moving odds, and settlement without a centralized book.

Advantages

  • Parimutuel math — all stakes go into one pot per wager; implied odds reflect aggregate sentiment, not a house line.
  • ERC-20 collateral — use standard tokens (e.g. USDC) per wager; amounts are enforced by contract logic.
  • Paramutuel deployments — classic simplicity, expressive bitmask ticket rules, and freeform for open-ended answers with on-chain hashing.
  • Composable lifecycle — factory-deployed wagers with configurable closers, resolution windows, and fee recipients (treasury + extras).
  • Live wager list — search and explore wagers without scraping the chain yourself.
  • Base-first — low-friction L2 for everyday wallet use.

Use cases

  • Prop and event wagers — sports, elections, product launches, KPI bets between teams or communities.
  • Long-horizon forecasts — templates for extended betting and resolution windows.
  • Closer-managed windows — authority-driven close when fixed deadlines are a poor fit.
  • Charity or beneficiary splits — fee configuration supports directing a share of the pot to treasuries or named recipients (within protocol caps).
  • Integrators — builders can use the same public wager data that powers this site; see About → Developers.
