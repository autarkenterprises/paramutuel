Assisted betting (in development)

The goal here is the same as on Propose a Wager: you should not have to coordinate ETH for gas and ERC-20 allowance as separate mental models for every bet. ADR-0006 / ADR-0007 describe assisted execution and permit paths on top of unchanged contracts.

Today, search results and Stake with wallet use a standard wallet flow (gas + approve where required) until the gateway ships. See assisted-ux-roadmap.md.

Place a Bet

Stake with wallet Paste a wager address or open a shared ?wager=0x… link.

By default only open wagers are listed. Search by wager address, proposition, outcome text (enumerated markets), protocol version (e.g. v2, freeform), proposer, resolver, factory, tx hash, or other indexed fields. Results refresh as you type; open any row to load the staking flow.
