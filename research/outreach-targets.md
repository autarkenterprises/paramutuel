Targets are grouped by category and scored on three axes:
- **Relevance** (R): how well their content/audience aligns with prop betting
- **Receptivity** (X): likelihood of responding and engaging (based on size, crypto-readiness, existing prediction behavior)
- **Market size** (M): reach and potential volume impact

Scale: 1-5 each. **Priority score = R + X + M** (max 15).

## Recommended outreach sequence

### Phase 0: Infrastructure (do now, before contacting anyone)

1. List MCP server on **Official MCP Registry** and **Smithery**
2. Apply for **Base grants** / builder program
3. Launch **Farcaster channel**
4. Publish **LangChain** and **CrewAI** tool wrappers
5. Submit PR to **modelcontextprotocol/servers**

### Phase 1: Machine distribution (weeks 1-2)

Contact **Olas/Polystrat**, **Virtuals Protocol**, and **AIXBT** about integration. These are technical partnerships that don't require marketing polish — they need working code and API docs, which already exist.

### Phase 2: Crypto-native media (weeks 2-4)

Pitch **Bankless**, **The Defiant**, and **Unchained** for interviews/features. Prepare a compelling demo: a live market about a trending crypto topic, with real testnet bets.

### Phase 3: Creator onboarding (weeks 3-6)

White-glove onboard **dvces**, **CryptoStache**, and 2-3 poker streamers (**ZerosPoker**, **Fintan Hand**, **Spraggy**). Provide testnet wallets, pre-funded markets, and a streamer kit.

### Phase 4: Sports & prediction personalities (weeks 4-8)

Approach **Calling Our Shot**, **PropBetGuy**, **Rex Woodbury**, and **Metaculus**. These require mainnet to be live and at least one successful creator use case as social proof.

### Phase 5: Aspirational / PR targets (launch week)

Time outreach to **Balaji Srinivasan**, **Scott Galloway**, and **Casey Newton** with the launch announcement. Frame around the narrative: "Remember that $1M Twitter bet? Now anyone can do this, on-chain, in 30 seconds."

## Appendix A — Competitive landscape (social/prop betting)

Understanding existing players is critical for positioning outreach. These are the closest competitors — the protocol must differentiate on decentralization, prop bet flexibility, and machine-native access.

| Competitor | Type | Chain/Platform | Differentiator | Weakness vs. us |
|-----------|------|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Polymarket** | Prediction market | Polygon | $3.7B+ monthly volume, Nate Silver advisor | Binary yes/no format only; not prop/paramutuel; US re-entry via license |
| **Kalshi** | Regulated prediction market | Centralized | $11B valuation, CFTC-regulated | Centralized, KYC-heavy, US-only, not crypto-native |
| **Limitless** | Prediction market | Base | $789M+ volume, largest on Base | Prediction market format, not paramutuel prop bets |
| **Azuro Protocol** | Sports betting infra | Multi-EVM | $358M volume, 27 front-end apps | Sports-focused infrastructure layer, not social/prop |
| **Overtime Markets** | Sports betting | Optimism/Base (Thales) | Established DeFi sports book | Sports-only, not general prop bets |
| **BettorEdge** | Social P2P betting | Centralized app | 45+ US states, no house edge | Centralized, not on-chain, no machine API |
| **Kutt** | Social betting | Centralized app | Any verifiable event, 3% fee | Centralized, no smart contract transparency |
| **SocialBets** | P2P betting | Ethereum | Smart contract wagers on any event | Closest competitor; less polished, smaller community |
| **Mevu** | P2P betting | Ethereum | Bet on virtually anything | Early stage, limited traction |
| **Kryptium** | P2P betting | Ethereum | Decentralized, no bookmaker | Low activity, older project |

**Our positioning**: The only protocol purpose-built for multi-outcome paramutuel prop bets on Base, with first-class MCP server integration for AI agent access. Unlike prediction markets (binary yes/no), we support N-outcome propositions with paramutuel pooling. Unlike centralized social betting apps, we're fully on-chain and programmable.
